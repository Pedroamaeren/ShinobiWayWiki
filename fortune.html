Fortune Trader

The Fortune Trader is a SHOP NPC that sells 2 Spins for Ryo. The price varies depending on your level, with the cost increasing as your level increases.

After the purchase, there is a 24-hour cooldown before you can buy them again.

At Level 250, the price reaches 325,000 Ryo for 2 Spins.
