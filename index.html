Welcome to ShinobiWayWiki.

Shinobi Way is an action RPG developed by Ouro Games on the Roblox platform. Inspired by the Naruto universe, the game was officially released on April 25, 2026. The main focus is on character progression through missions and combat using jutsus, bloodlines, weapons, and diverse things. (This is an unofficial wiki.)
